Formula used: single-phase runs use VD = 2 × I × R × D / 1000; three-phase runs use VD = √3 × I × R × D / 1000, where R is per-conductor resistance divided by the number of parallel sets, D is one-way distance in feet, and I is load current in amps. Resistance values are DC, uncoated conductor figures from NEC Chapter 9, Table 8 — adequate for resistive heater loads at unity power factor, where conductor reactance is negligible. For loads with power factor below roughly 0.9, use NEC Table 9 effective impedance instead.
